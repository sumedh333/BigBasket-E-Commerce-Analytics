# BigBasket E-Commerce Analytics – Product Assortment & Brand Insights

## Overview
This project analyzes BigBasket’s e-commerce product catalog to understand **category-wise assortment depth, brand dominance, and pricing distribution**, with the goal of supporting **assortment planning, private-label strategy, and catalog optimization**.

---

## Dataset
- **Source**: BigBasket product catalog dataset  
- **Records**: ~27,500 products  
- **Key Attributes**: Category, brand, price, discount, ratings  

---

## Analysis Focus
The analysis focuses on:
- Category-level product distribution  
- Brand dominance and private-label presence  
- High-level pricing structure across the catalog  

---

## Exploratory Data Analysis (Key Findings)

### 1. Brand-wise Product Assortment
![Brand-wise Product Assortment](visuals/brand_product_distribution.png)

**Insight:**  
Private-label brands such as **Fresho, BB Royal, and BB Home** dominate the catalog, indicating a strong in-house brand strategy and reduced reliance on third-party brands.

---

### 2. Category-wise Product Assortment
![Category-wise Product Assortment](visuals/category_product_count.png)

**Insight:**  
Categories like **Beauty & Hygiene** and **Gourmet & World Food** show deep assortment, while **Baby Care** and **Fruits & Vegetables** have relatively limited product depth.
